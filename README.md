## Client Brief

We regularly organize events such as workshops, training sessions, networking events and company activities.

At the moment, information about our events and the people attending them is managed using different methods.

As the number of events grows, we would like to explore a digital solution that can help us manage our events and the people who attend them.

We need a better way to organize event information, manage registrations and understand who is attending each event.

We are open to your team's recommendations on how the system should work.
